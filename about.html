Our Story

ETS Logistics LLC was founded with a simple goal: to give every driver the kind of support that big fleets take for granted. We started small, but our commitment to fair rates and honest business has helped us grow into a trusted name in American trucking.

Our Mission

To empower owner-operators and small fleets by providing top-paying loads, 24/7 real human support, and transparent communication. We handle the paperwork so you can focus on driving.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and driver-focused dispatch company in the United States, known for integrity, excellence, and building relationships that last beyond just one job.
