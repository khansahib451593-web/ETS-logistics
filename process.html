OUR SIMPLE PROCESS

How We Keep You Moving

Six easy steps to start maximizing your earnings with our expert dispatch team.

01

Contact ETS Logistics

Give us a call or fill out our quick dispatch form. Tell us about your truck, your equipment, and your preferred lanes.

02

Share Your Info

Send us your MC/DOT authority, W-9, and insurance certificate. We'll build your professional carrier profile instantly.

03

Meet Your Dispatcher

Get connected with a dedicated, real human dispatcher who understands your goals and home-time requirements.

04

Find Suitable Loads

We search top load boards and our direct broker network to find the highest-paying freight on your exact lanes.

05

We Book & Negotiate

We negotiate every single rate. You review the rate confirmation and give the final approval before we book.

06

Keep Moving

You drive and earn. We handle broker calls, paperwork, invoicing, and 24/7 support until you get paid.
